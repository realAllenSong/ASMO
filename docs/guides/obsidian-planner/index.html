Days and notes in one app, for Obsidian users

Updated 2026-11-22

If you keep notes in Obsidian but plan your days somewhere else, ASMO puts both in one Mac app. Its notes are a folder of Markdown files, so an Obsidian vault works as it is: tables, callouts, formulas, diagrams and Excalidraw drawings render in place. Your days live beside them, and to-dos go to Apple Calendar and Reminders, and from there to your iPhone. It is free and open source.

What carries over from Obsidian

  • The same files. Notes are plain Markdown in a folder; open your vault and keep using Obsidian on the same files.
  • Rich blocks. Tables, callouts, inline and block formulas, and mermaid diagrams are drawn in place.
  • Excalidraw. Drawings are saved the way Obsidian's Excalidraw plugin saves them, so the same file opens in both.
  • Git sync. A vault that is a Git repository is committed, pulled and pushed by itself, so Obsidian on your phone sees what you wrote.

Getting started

  1. Download ASMO and open it.
  2. Choose your Obsidian vault as ASMO's notes folder.
  3. Write today's plan on the day page; the to-dos appear in Calendar and Reminders.

FAQ

Can ASMO open my Obsidian vault?

Yes. Notes are a folder of Markdown files, so point ASMO at your vault and both apps work on the same files.

Does it replace Obsidian?

It does not have to. Many people keep Obsidian for writing and use ASMO for days and to-dos on the same vault.

How does it sync to my iPhone?

Days sync through Apple Calendar and Reminders on your iCloud; notes in a Git repository sync through Git.
